# Garden film

`garden-film.mp4` and `garden-poster.jpg` are edited from **Sun Rays Coming Through the Trees** by Matthias Groeneveld on Pexels:

- Source: https://www.pexels.com/video/sun-rays-coming-through-the-trees-6327822/
- License: https://www.pexels.com/license/ (free commercial use and modification)
- Edits: silent, resized, compressed, and mirrored in time for a seamless 12-second loop; poster extracted from the edited film.

The botanical illustration and animation are original SVG/CSS created for MindGarden. The film is self-hosted; visitors make no requests to Pexels.
